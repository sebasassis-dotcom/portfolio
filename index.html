About

There are two types of copywriters.
(According to David Ogilvy.)
Poets and killers.
The poets do it for the magic.
The killers do it for the sales.
The poets see the ad as an end.
The killers see it as a means to an end.
There is, however, a third type:
The ones who are both poets and killers.
The ones who create magic and generate results.
That's the kind of copywriter I try to be.
